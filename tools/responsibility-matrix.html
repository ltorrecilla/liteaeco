Responsibility Matrix

Define roles and responsibilities for tasks

What this tool does
Make sure your team is working well together and that your projects are on schedule. Easily identify who is responsible, accountable, supportive, consulted or informed for each task or project deliverables, ensuring clear ownership.

This tool uses an Universal Excel files so you can load your contact back and in other webapps from this website.

- Keep it simple, avoid having too many "Consulted" roles to prevent delays.
- Only one Accountability A per task to ensure clarity.
- Review regularly, update the matrix if team members or roles change during the project.
