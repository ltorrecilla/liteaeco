Monte Carlo Simulator

Probabilistic Schedule Analysis

What this tool does

Performs a quantitative risk analysis technique by modeling thousands of possible scenarios to forecast potential project schedules. By combining task or phase durations with probability distributions (like triangular or PERT), calculating the statistical probability of meeting specific deadlines.

You can load external project timelines and gantt charts as Excel files either from this webapp or other LiteAECO tools, as well as MS Project .xml files.
